Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This desktop video editor ("the app") runs entirely on your own computer. It is built and used by its owner for editing video and posting finished exports to video services the owner signs in to.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no server, no analytics and no account system. Your media files, projects and edits stay on your computer.

Sign-in to video services

When you connect a service such as YouTube, Vimeo or TikTok, the app opens that service's own sign-in page in your browser. The service issues an access token, which the app stores in your operating system's credential store on your computer. The app uses that token only to upload the videos you choose to post and to read the processing status of those uploads. It does not read your other content, contacts or activity. When you connect TikTok, the app reads your basic profile (display name, avatar and account ID) to show which account is connected; it is not stored anywhere else.

What leaves your computer

Only the video file you choose to post, with the title, description, tags and privacy setting you enter, and only to the service you selected. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Removing access

You can sign out inside the app at any time, which deletes the stored token, and you can revoke the app's access from the video service's own account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner through the repository that hosts this page.
